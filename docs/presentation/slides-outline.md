# Symphony — Private AI for Small Businesses

## 1. The Opportunity

- Small and mid-sized businesses need AI benefits without compromising sensitive data or control.
- Existing choices force a trade-off between security, cost, and capability.

## 2. Our Solution

- Symphony delivers a private, managed AI platform across on-premises and customer cloud environments.
- Businesses gain secure everyday AI, controlled access to advanced models, and a clear path to scale.

## 3. Product Platform

- **Music Box:** On-premises AI appliance for private local inference.
- **Ensemble:** Private cloud AI environment, deployed within the customer’s AWS account.
- **Orchestra:** Secure routing to frontier models for workloads requiring greater capability.
- **Maestro:** The unified assistant experience for employees.

## 4. How It Works

- Symphony assesses prompt sensitivity and complexity.
- Appropriate requests remain local; that may include tools or sensitive data requests route securely to private cloud or approved frontier models.
- Centralized controls give businesses visibility, governance, and policy enforcement.

## 5. Customer Value

- Keep business data under customer control.
- Reduce risk through private deployment and governed AI access.
- Deliver faster, more cost-effective AI for everyday work.
- Expand capabilities without replacing the platform.

## 6. Target Customer

- Small and mid-sized businesses with sensitive data, limited internal AI resources, and a need for practical deployment support.

## 7. Business Model

- Upfront deployment and on-premises hardware.
- Recurring subscription for cloud support, maintenance, security updates, and platform upgrades.

## 8. Why Symphony

- One partner for private AI strategy, deployment, and ongoing operations.
- A flexible architecture that balances privacy, performance, and access to innovation.
